Privacy Policy

Grid Block Shift · Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Grid Block Shift (the "App"), published by Daynix Studio, handles information when you use it. By installing or using the App, you agree to the terms of this policy.

At a glance: Grid Block Shift is a fully offline puzzle game. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. No account, no sign-in, no internet connection is required.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information from you. Specifically, Grid Block Shift does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details
  • Your location or device identifiers
  • Your contacts, photos, files, or any other content from your device
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostic information
  • Any form of advertising identifiers

2. Local Data on Your Device

To let you keep your puzzle progress, the App stores a small amount of information locally on your device only, using the standard Android preferences storage (SharedPreferences). This data stays on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is not accessible to Daynix Studio or any third party.

What is stored locally:

  • Levels you have completed, stars earned, and best move counts
  • Lifetime stats such as total moves and hints used
  • Achievements you have unlocked
  • Daily puzzle completion status for the current date
  • Your theme preference (Light or Dark) and whether you have finished the onboarding

You can clear all of this at any time from the in-app Settings → Reset Progress option, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Internet, Accounts, and Backend Services

Grid Block Shift is designed to work entirely offline. The App does not require an internet connection, does not sign you in to any account, and does not use any backend server, cloud service, or online API.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, social, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected in the first place.

5. Advertisements

Grid Block Shift does not display advertisements.

6. Permissions

The App does not request any sensitive runtime permissions. It does not access the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage files, or any other protected resource.

7. Children's Privacy

Grid Block Shift is suitable for all ages. Because the App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, it does not knowingly collect any information from children. Parents and guardians can let children use the App without concern that personal data will be gathered.

8. Data Security

All game data is stored only on your device using Android's standard application sandbox. Since no information is ever transmitted off your device, there is no network-based risk to your data from using this App.

9. Data Retention

Any locally stored data stays on your device until you clear it via Settings → Reset Progress or uninstall the App. Daynix Studio does not retain any copies of your data because none is ever collected or transmitted.

10. Your Rights

Because no personal information is collected by the App or Daynix Studio, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You remain in full control of the on-device game data at all times through Android's app settings and the in-app Reset Progress option.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or in applicable laws. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes may also be communicated through the App's store listing.

12. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is provided by Daynix Studio and is intended to comply with the requirements of the Google Play Store and applicable privacy regulations for mobile applications that do not collect personal data.
